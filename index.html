03 / Projects

MSc Dissertation · Threat Detection

Snort Community Ruleset: ATT&CK Threat Coverage Analysis

How much of MITRE ATT&CK does a widely used IDS ruleset actually detect? A five-stage Python framework parses all 4,017 Snort 3 Community rules, maps them to ATT&CK (STIX 2.1, v19.1), then uses an LLM to classify the rules that traditional mapping couldn't.

  • Rule-based mapping 430
  • LLM-classified 3,110
  • Still unmapped 477
10.7%of rules map to ATT&CK with rule-based evidence
86.7%of unmapped rules classified by the LLM
94unique ATT&CK techniques proposed by the LLM, vs 34 before
34rules citing revoked ATT&CK technique IDs
  • Direct and heuristic mapping from rule evidence: msg, content, PCRE, references, service and ports.
  • LLM classification with NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra (550B), plus a manually adjudicated blind validation across Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini.
  • Coverage analysis of modern threats (ransomware, DNS abuse) and detection-difficulty tiering.
  • Python
  • MITRE ATT&CK
  • STIX 2.1
  • Snort 3
  • LLMs
  • Detection engineering

FaceGuard: AI Security Assessment

A full attack chain against a CNN facial-recognition access-control system, from model theft to privilege escalation, followed by the blue-team fix.

  • Black-box model stealing: a proxy CNN replicating the victim at ~98% accuracy.
  • FGSM and PGD attacks with ~99% transfer success, plus a targeted impersonation attack.
  • Adversarial training (Madry et al.) to harden it as Robust_FaceGuard.
